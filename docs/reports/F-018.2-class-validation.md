## 3. Review of Low-Volume Classes (`sorry` and `stop`)

### 3.1 Class: `sorry` (6 clips)
- **Source Breakdown:**
  - CISLR: 4 clips (`00002`, `00031`, `00049`, `00072`) — 480x480 resolution, 30 FPS, durations 2.97s to 3.53s.
  - ISLRTC Dictionary: 2 clips (`00636__Sorry`, `00637__Sorry_(Sign_2)`) — 854x480 resolution, 30 FPS.
- **Linguistic Finding:**
  - In Indian Sign Language, two accepted lexical variants exist for "sorry" documented by ISLRTC:
    - *Variant 1:* Closed fist touching chest with circular apology motion.
    - *Variant 2:* Two-handed / ear-touching repentance gesture common in Indian schools for the deaf.
  - Both variants represent authentic native ISL communication.
- **Decision:** **APPROVED WITH LOW DATA**. All 6 clips approved for landmark extraction. Data augmentation (speed variations, slight landmark translation) required during training.

### 3.2 Class: `stop` (4 clips)
- **Source Breakdown:**
  - CISLR: 2 clips (`00021`, `00022`) — 480x480 resolution, 30 FPS, durations 2.53s and 4.33s.
  - ISLRTC Dictionary: 2 clips (`00631__Cease_Discontinue_Halt_Stop`, `00639__Stop`) — 854x480 resolution, 30 FPS.
- **Search for Additional Samples:**
  - Entire 40-word corpus was scanned for synonyms (`halt`, `cease`, `stand`, `wait`, `pause`).
  - No additional `stop` clips exist in the current aggregate repository.
- **Decision:** **NEEDS MORE DATA**. 3 clips approved for initial baseline; supplemental custom recordings recommended in F-019.

## 4. Analysis of Flagged Stop Clip

- **Clip Path:** `stop/stop__ISLRTC_dictionary__00631__Cease_Discontinue_Halt_Stop.mp4`
- **Original Source Label:** `Cease_Discontinue_Halt_Stop` (ISLRTC Dictionary entry under "C").
- **Inspection Findings:**
  - The video is 4.30 seconds long, 854x480 at 30 FPS, 129 frames.
  - Unlike standard conversational "stop" (single open palm held forward), this video demonstrates a formal compound sign combining two hands crossing and terminating downward (used in formal contexts for "cease / halt operation").
  - While linguistically valid in formal dictionary taxonomy, its movement profile differs significantly from everyday conversational palm-out "stop" signs present in CISLR (`stop__CISLR__00021` and `00022`).
- **Resolution:**
  - Manifest validation status: `NEEDS_MANUAL_VERIFICATION`.
  - Training split flag: `include_in_mvp_train: false`.
  - Excluded from initial training pipeline to prevent false negative rate inflation on standard webcam stop gestures.

## 6. Sign Verification Limitations (ISL vs ASL Nuances)

During inspection, key distinctions between Indian Sign Language (ISL) and American Sign Language (ASL) were catalogued to guide model evaluation:

1. **`thank you`:** ASL uses flat hand touching lips/chin and extending outward. ISL commonly utilizes a two-handed gesture (one horizontal base palm, one tapping or moving upwards) or a polite palms-pressed gesture depending on region.
2. **`please`:** ASL rubs flat hand circularly on sternum. ISL uses an open palm with gentle wrist rotation or palms folded in namaste-adjacent posture.
3. **`water`:** ASL signs "W" handshape tapping chin. ISL uses a cupped hand tilted towards mouth (mimicking drinking from a vessel).
4. **`sorry`:** ASL rubs "A" fist on chest. ISL recognizes both chest circle and ear-holding/head tilt gesture.

Models trained on this dataset will recognize authentic ISL gestures rather than western ASL conventions.

## 7. Machine-Readable Manifest Structure

The verified manifest has been exported to:
- `datasets/manifests/isl_10class_manifest.json`
- `datasets/manifests/isl_10class_manifest.csv`

### Manifest Fields
- `video_id`: Unique identifier (`ISL10_0001` through `ISL10_0188`).
- `class_label`: Target class label (`hello`, `thank you`, `sorry`, etc.).
- `normalized_word`: Normalized text string.
- `original_label`: Exact label in source dataset.
- `source_dataset`: Upstream source (`ISL500`, `INCLUDE`, `CISLR`, `ISLRTC_dictionary`).
- `signer_id`: Signer / user identifier.
- `relative_video_path`: Path relative to dataset root.
- `absolute_video_path`: Full canonical disk path.
- `file_exists`: Boolean verification (all 188 confirmed `True`).
- `fps`: Capture frame rate (30.0).
- `resolution`: Spatial dimensions (`854x480` or `480x480`).
- `duration_sec`: Duration in seconds.
- `quality_score`: Upstream confidence score (0.85 - 1.0).
- `upstream_review_status`: `accepted` or `Needs Manual Review`.
- `validation_status`: `APPROVED` or `NEEDS_MANUAL_VERIFICATION`.
- `include_in_mvp_train`: `true` (187 clips) or `false` (1 clip).
- `notes`: Specific validation notes (e.g., variant notes, aliasing details).
