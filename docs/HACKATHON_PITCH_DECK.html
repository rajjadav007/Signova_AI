THE HUMAN PROBLEM • COMMUNICATION BARRIER 02 / 12

Communication should never be a barrier.

Millions of individuals who communicate through Indian Sign Language encounter an invisible wall in daily life.

• Daily Exchanges: Simple conversations become hesitant exchanges of gestures or scribbled notes.
• Public Services: Banks, transit, and civic counters require third-party escorts or remain inaccessible.
• Healthcare: Patients lose privacy and clinical nuance when interpreters are absent.
• Dignity & Autonomy: Constant dependence on others limits independence in private matters.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY: ZERO RAW PIXEL DEPENDENCY

Rather than feeding raw RGB video into convolutional networks—which overfit to background, lighting, and skin tone—Signova AI strips visual noise immediately. We convert motion into 75 skeletal landmark coordinates, letting the model learn pure movement geometry. The result: <20ms inference on a standard laptop CPU.

NEURAL ARCHITECTURE • 25-CLASS SENTENCE PIPELINE 08 / 12

Understanding movement, not just images.

SPATIAL FEATURE VECTOR DESIGN
• Total Landmarks / Frame: 75 Keypoints
• Upper Body Pose: 33 Keypoints (Shoulders, Arms, Spine)
• Left Hand: 21 Keypoints (Fingers, Palm, Wrist)
• Right Hand: 21 Keypoints (Fingers, Palm, Wrist)
• Channels / Landmark: 4 → X, Y, Z, Visibility
• Feature Vector / Frame: 75 × 4 = 300 Dimensions
• Temporal Sequence: Resampled to fixed 32 / 64 frames
END-TO-END NEURAL ARCHITECTURE
1 Input Sequence ➔ Sequential video frames [T × 300]
2 MediaPipe Holistic ➔ 75 landmarks × 4 channels = 300-dim per frame
3 Normalization ➔ Centre on torso, scale-invariant to camera distance
4 BiGRU Encoder ➔ 2-layer Bidirectional GRU | Hidden=128 | Dropout=0.3
5 Self-Attention ➔ Weights critical temporal inflection frames per sign
6 Classifier Head ➔ Linear projection → 25-class Softmax output

DIFFERENTIATORS • SPATIAL + TEMPORAL DYNAMICS 09 / 12

Signs are movement, not just shapes.

Spatial Information
Captures precise 3D positions of finger joints, wrist orientation, and upper-torso reference points across every frame.
Temporal Information
Models velocity, acceleration, and trajectory arcs. Many signs share identical hand shapes yet differ entirely in motion pattern.
Dual Recognition Pipelines
DTW-KNN for fast isolated word identification. BiGRU-Attention for continuous multi-sign phrase sequences. Each tuned independently.
Closed-Vocabulary Honesty
Strict confidence thresholds reject out-of-vocabulary inputs rather than producing false translations—engineering integrity over inflated claims.
